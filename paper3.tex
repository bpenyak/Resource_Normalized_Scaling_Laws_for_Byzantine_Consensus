\abstract{%
\textbf{Problem.} Sizing a permissioned BFT validator set for e-governance
must reconcile falling consensus throughput with rising fault-detection power
as~$\n$ grows. Published single-factor exponents are hard to interpret as
protocol properties unless the concurrency path is reported.
\textbf{Contributions.}
A reproducible engineering methodology for resource-normalized calibration and
capacity planning:
\emph{method} --- resource-normalized measurement separating consensus cost from
host contention;
\emph{analysis} --- identifiability relation
$\ahat_{m}\xrightarrow{\Prob}\gamma\lambda-\beta$ under a bi-factor model;
\emph{engineering} --- chance-constrained sizing with feasible set
$[\nmin,\nmax]$;
\emph{empirics} --- coverage-checked prediction intervals on public commodity
CI.
\textbf{Scope.} Four-core CI ($\n\le\resNmaxMeasured$), emulated RTT, and
omission/duplication injection; fitted coefficients are deployment-specific and
intended for recalibration under matching conditions.
