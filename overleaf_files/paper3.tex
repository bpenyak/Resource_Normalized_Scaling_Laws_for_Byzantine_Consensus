\abstract{%
\textbf{Problem.} Sizing a permissioned BFT validator set for national-scale
e-governance must reconcile degrading consensus throughput with improving
fault-detection power as~$\n$ grows; without reporting the concurrency path,
published single-factor exponents are difficult to interpret as intrinsic
protocol properties.
\textbf{Contributions.}
An engineering methodology for resource-normalized capacity planning:
\emph{method} --- resource-normalized measurement isolating consensus cost from
host contention;
\emph{analysis} --- identifiability relation
$\ahat_{m}\xrightarrow{\Prob}\gamma\lambda-\beta$ along a concurrency path;
\emph{engineering} --- chance-constrained sizing with feasible set
$[\nmin,\nmax]$;
\emph{empirics} --- coverage-checked prediction intervals on public CI.
\textbf{Validation.} A four-core commodity-CI campaign covers
$\n\le\resNmaxMeasured$, concurrency up to~$64$, emulated RTT, and
omission/duplication fault injection; fitted coefficients are
deployment-specific.

Методика ресурсно-нормованого планування потужності для визначення розміру
множини валідаторів BFT-мережі в електронному урядуванні: пропускна здатність
