\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\paragraph{What is established.}
Under the unsaturated bi-factor model and stated path assumptions, the
single-factor OLS exponent satisfies
$\ahat_{m}\xrightarrow{\Prob}\gamma\lambda-\beta$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:identifiability}). Opposing monotone constraints yield a
closed integer feasibility window (Proposition~\ref{prop:window}). The
contribution is a reproducible engineering methodology for resource-normalized
capacity planning: isolate a consensus exponent under \RNM{}, then size the
validator set under calibrated chance constraints.

\paragraph{What the experiments indicate.}
Under \RNM{} on four-core commodity CI:
$\beta=\resBeta\pm\resBetaSE$, $\gamma=\resGamma\pm\resGammaSE$; path
re-sampling is consistent with $\gamma\lambda-\beta$ within the investigated
configuration; coefficients at $\q=0.25$; coverage meets nominal levels within
the calibrated range; AUC$\,=\,\resAuc$ defaults the window to throughput.
WAN RTT raises~$\beta$ from $\resBetaRttZero$ to $\resBetaRttHigh$; doubled
demand or WAN $\beta$ at fixed $\kappaScale$ empties the window. Numerical
results are deployment-specific.

\paragraph{What remains open.}
Equivocation-capable instrumentation; multi-client numerical checks; latency or
finality constraints inside the window; online recalibration of~$\beta$.
