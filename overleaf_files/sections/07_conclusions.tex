\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

\paragraph{What is established.}
Along a concurrency path of slope~$\lambda$, the single-factor OLS scaling
exponent satisfies $\ahat_{m}\xrightarrow{\Prob}\gamma\lambda-\beta$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:identifiability}). Opposing monotone constraints yield a
closed integer feasibility window (Proposition~\ref{prop:window}). The
contribution is a rigorous capacity-planning methodology: isolate the consensus
exponent under \RNM{}, then size the validator set under calibrated chance
constraints.

\paragraph{What is confirmed experimentally.}
Under \RNM{} on four-core CI:
$\beta=\resBeta\pm\resBetaSE$, $\gamma=\resGamma\pm\resGammaSE$; path
re-sampling tracks $\ahat_{m}\xrightarrow{\Prob}\gamma\lambda-\beta$;
coefficients at $\q=0.25$; coverage meets nominal levels; AUC$\,=\,\resAuc$
defaults the window to throughput. WAN RTT raises~$\beta$ from
$\resBetaRttZero$ to $\resBetaRttHigh$. Substituting the same coefficients
shows that doubled demand or WAN $\beta$ at fixed $\kappaScale$ empties the
window.

\paragraph{What remains open.}
Equivocation-capable instrumentation; multi-client numerical checks; latency or
finality constraints inside the window; online recalibration of~$\beta$.
