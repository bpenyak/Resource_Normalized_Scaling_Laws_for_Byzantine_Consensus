\section{Experimental validation}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup and design}

The campaign runs in a public continuous-integration workflow on a four-core,
16~GB hosted runner. Each job provisions a Hyperledger Besu~\cite{besu} QBFT
network of~$\n$ validators under the \RNM{} protocol
(Definition~\ref{def:rnm}); a closed-loop generator drives~$\cc$ concurrent
submitters and records steady-state committed throughput after warm-up.

Faults are injected with kernel traffic control (packet loss and duplication) on
designated validators, realizing the omission class of
Definition~\ref{def:faults} but not equivocation. The detector polls signer
metrics over a window of~$\w$ rounds to estimate $\pd$, $\pf$, $\rho$ and the
ROC; the Youden threshold is used in Section~\ref{sec:case}.

Measured groups: \textbf{X1} sweeps $\n\in\{4,\dots,16\}$ at $\cc=16$;
\textbf{X2} tests $\q$-invariance at three quotas; \textbf{X3} sweeps
$\cc\in\{1,\dots,64\}$ at $\n=7$; \textbf{X4} adds emulated RTT;
\textbf{X5} injects omission/duplication at faulty fraction $\phi=0.2$.
Cross-client replication on a second QBFT-family implementation (planned as
\textbf{X9}/GoQuorum) is \emph{out of scope} for this paper: Theorem~\ref{thm:identifiability}
is a statement about least squares, not about any one client, and the numerical
coefficients below are reported as Besu/QBFT-specific by design. Post-hoc:
\textbf{X3b} re-samples along
concurrency paths $\lambda\in\{0,0.5,1,2\}$; \textbf{X6} is a discrete-event
simulation used only for qualitative extrapolation beyond the measured range
(not as a measurement); \textbf{X7} predictive coverage on $\resHoldoutN$;
\textbf{X8} sizing ablation. In total $\resTotalRuns$ successful records,
$\approx\resCoreHours$ core-hours, $\n_{\max}=\resNmaxMeasured$.

\subsection{Results}

Joint OLS on the reference-quota calibration subset yields
$\beta=\resBeta\pm\resBetaSE$ and $\gamma=\resGamma\pm\resGammaSE$
($R^{2}=\resRsq$); remaining parameters appear in Table~\ref{tab:results}.
Under \RNM{} one must have $\beta>0$; a single-factor fit along $\lambda=1$
instead gives $\ahat=\resAlphaHat$ against the predicted
$\gamma\lambda-\beta=\resAlphaPred$.

Figure~\ref{fig:confounding} confirms that estimated exponents track the
predicted line. The partial $F$-test for quota\,$\times\log\n$ interaction
rejects exact $\q$-invariance ($p=\resQinvP$), so coefficients are reported at
the reference quota $\q=0.25$ only; $\beta$ rises from $\resBetaRttZero$ at
zero RTT to $\resBetaRttHigh$ under elevated latency (X4).
